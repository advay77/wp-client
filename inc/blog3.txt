Does Packaging Compliance Matter for Amazon in 2026?

Amazon used to offer a safety net: if a shipment showed up with a missing barcode or an unbagged item, Amazon would prep it for a fee. That option disappeared on January 1, 2026, when Amazon ended its US FBA prep and labeling services entirely.

Every poly bag, barcode, box, and warning label now has to be correct before the shipment leaves your facility or your prep center.

The core specifications themselves haven't changed dramatically , they're largely the same rules Amazon has used for years. What changed is enforcement.

Inbound defect fees for issues like missing or unscannable barcodes have increased substantially, and a shipment with multiple packaging errors can now cost hundreds of dollars in fees on top of the delay.

Getting the details right the first time is now meaningfully cheaper than fixing them after the fact.

Poly Bag Requirements

Poly bags are required for many loose items, apparel, and anything with small parts that could separate or contaminate other inventory. The core specifications:

1. Minimum thickness: 1.5 mil, and bags must be transparent (except for adult products, which require opaque or black bags)
2. Sealing: Bags must be fully sealed and fit the product without excessive loose material , a bag shouldn't hang more than about 3 inches past the product
3. Suffocation warning: Required on any poly bag with an opening of 5 inches or more, measured flat. The required font size scales with the bag's combined length and width. Larger bags need larger warning text
4. Barcode visibility: The barcode must remain scannable either through the bag or via a label applied to the outside

Example: a cotton t-shirt folded and sealed in a poly bag needs both a visible suffocation warning (since a folded shirt typically creates an opening wider than 5 inches) and a barcode placed on a flat, scannable section of the bag.

Barcode and Labeling Requirements

Every unit needs a single, scannable barcode , either an Amazon-issued FNSKU or an eligible manufacturer barcode, depending on your Brand Registry status. (Read our article on FNSKU vs UPC for how to determine which applies to you).

Barcodes must be placed on a flat surface where a scanner can read them cleanly; curved surfaces, seams, and low-contrast backgrounds are common causes of scan failures at receiving.

Category-Specific Packaging Notes

Certain product types carry additional requirements beyond the general rules:

1. Liquids need double-sealed, leak-proof packaging
2. Glass and ceramic items need bubble wrap and box cushioning sufficient to survive standard warehouse handling
3. Expiration-dated products (food, supplements, topicals) need date labeling in the correct format and font size , covered in detail in our [INTERNAL LINK OPPORTUNITY: Amazon FBA Expiration Date Requirements]
4. Adult products require opaque or black poly bags, with the same barcode visibility and suffocation warning rules still applying
5. Bundled or kitted sets need shrink wrap or another method to keep the set together under a single barcode

Sustainable and Frustration-Free Packaging Options

Amazon also offers a Frustration-Free Packaging (FFP) program, which can reduce prep requirements and fees for products that ship in their own retail packaging without additional bagging or boxing. Products enrolled in FFP still need to meet Amazon's durability and labeling standards, but they can skip some of the poly-bagging steps required for standard packaging.

A Practical Example

A seller ships a set of ceramic mugs bundled as a 4-pack.

Each mug needs to be individually wrapped to survive shipping, the 4-pack needs to be boxed with enough internal cushioning to pass a drop test, and the outer box needs a single scannable barcode representing the bundled set , not four separate barcodes.

If the seller instead ships each mug in its original retail packaging that already meets Amazon's protection standards, the set may qualify for reduced poly-bagging requirements under FFP.

Frequently Asked Questions

What thickness do Amazon FBA poly bags need to be?
Amazon requires poly bags to be at least 1.5 mil thick and transparent, except for adult products, which require opaque or black bags.

When does a poly bag need a suffocation warning?
Any poly bag with an opening of 5 inches or more, measured flat, needs a suffocation warning printed on the bag or attached as a label, with font size scaling to the bag's overall dimensions.

What is the maximum weight for an Amazon FBA box?

Does Amazon still fix packaging mistakes for sellers?
No. As of January 1, 2026, Amazon no longer offers in-house prep or packaging correction services in the US. Non-compliant shipments are now held, returned, or charged defect fees instead.

What is Frustration-Free Packaging (FFP)?
FFP is an Amazon program that lets qualifying products ship in their own retail packaging without additional poly bagging, provided the packaging meets Amazon's durability and labeling standards.

Do all items need a suffocation warning label?
No. Only poly bags with an opening of 5 inches or more require the warning. Boxed items and smaller bags below that threshold generally don't need it.

What causes barcode scan failures at Amazon receiving?
Common causes include placing barcodes on curved or textured surfaces, low print contrast, damaged labels, and using the wrong barcode type for your seller status.

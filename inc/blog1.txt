What is Amazon FBA Prep?

Amazon FBA prep is the work required before inventory reaches a fulfillment center. This includes the following:

1. Applying scannable barcodes (usually FNSKU labels)
2. Securing loose items, polybagging, bubble wrapping, bundling multipacks, and meeting category-specific packaging rules.

Since January 1, 2026, Amazon no longer performs this work in the U.S.

Thus, Sellers must prep themselves, use a prep center, or have suppliers ship FBA-ready units.

The Two 2026 Changes Every Seller Should Know

Two policy shifts reshaped prep for most U.S. sellers:

1. Amazon prep services ended (January 1, 2026)

Amazon no longer polybags, bubble wraps, labels, or bundles inventory on your behalf at U.S. fulfillment centers. This applies across FBA inbound paths, including Send to Amazon and Supply Chain by Amazon. Sellers who relied on Amazon to apply FNSKU labels must now print and apply labels themselves or outsource to a prep partner.

2. Commingling ended for most resellers (March 31, 2026)

Resellers who are not brand representatives in Brand Registry must use Amazon barcodes (FNSKU) on eligible FBA units, even when a manufacturer UPC is already on the package.

Brand-registered owners may still use manufacturer barcodes on eligible ASINs.

Verify your accountâ€™s barcode settings in Seller Central under Use Amazon barcodes.

Together, these changes mean most sellers now label every unit with an FNSKU before inbounding.

Practical Examples

Example 1: Wholesale reseller
You buy branded protein bars with UPCs already on each wrapper. After March 31, 2026, you print an FNSKU for each unit, cover the UPC completely, and ship cartons to the assigned FC. No Amazon relabeling at receiving.

Example 2: Apparel private label
T-shirts arrive from your manufacturer in loose bundles. Each unit needs a transparent poly bag with a suffocation warning (opening 5 inches or larger when laid flat), an FNSKU on the outside of the bag, and cartons that do not exceed weight limits.

Example 3: Fragile supplement bottle
Glass bottles need bubble wrap or rigid boxing so they survive FC handling. If you polybag the wrapped bottle, the expiration date and FNSKU must remain visible and scannable on the outermost layer.

Common Prep Mistakes That Cause Receiving Problems

1. Partial barcode coverage: FNSKU sticker placed over only part of the UPC; scanners read the wrong code.
2. Non-scannable labels: Smudged, wrinkled, or low-resolution barcodes.
3. Missing suffocation warnings: Poly bags with 5-inch+ openings and no compliant warning text.
4. Loose multipacks: Bundles that can separate in the FC.
5. Ignoring SKU-level prep flags: Assuming all items in a category share the same prep rules.
6. When in doubt, re-check Seller Central for the specific ASIN before shipping your next batch.

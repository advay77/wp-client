 Setting Up TikTok Shop Fulfillment: Step-by-Step 
 
 
Step 1: Confirm category access 
Supplements are often invite-only with lab reports, COAs, and label review. Beauty and personal care may require category qualification. Resolve gating before you stock inventory. 
 
 
Step 2: Choose your fulfillment model 
Map hero SKUs vs long-tail SKUs to FBT, TikTok Shipping, or seller shipping. Document who owns SLA risk for each. 
 
 
Step 3: Connect systems 
Integrate Seller Center with your OMS, 3PL WMS, or Shopify stack so orders flow automatically,manual CSV workflows break at viral volume. 
 
 
Step 4: Set cutoffs and scan discipline 
Define a daily pick/pack cutoff that guarantees carrier scans before SLA expiry. Photograph or log handoffs during ramp-up. 
 
 
Step 5: Run test orders 
Process test orders through each carrier path. Verify tracking uploads, scan events, and delivery milestones in Seller Center. 
 
 
 
 
Step 6: Plan for spikes 
Pre-negotiate surge capacity with your 3PL or temp labor. Viral demand is the defining TikTok operational risk. 
 
 
Step 7: Align multi-channel inventory 
If the same SKUs sell on Shopify and TikTok, define allocation rules and safety stock so TikTok spikes do not zero out DTC or marketplace listings.

Multi-Channel Inventory: What Not to Do 
 
 
Do not assume one undifferentiated inventory pool without rules. TikTok spikes can: 
 
 
- Drain units promised to Amazon FBA replenishment 
- Cause Shopify oversells 
- Push expiration-dated lots out of FEFO sequence 
 
 
Do define channel priorities, buffer stock, and which SKUs are TikTok-exclusive vs shared. For expiration-dated health and wellness products, lot-level tracking is not optional,it prevents shipping short-dated inventory because TikTok moved faster than expected.

Yes, when seller shipping is enabled for your account, a 3PL can pick, pack, and ship TikTok orders if it meets SLA timing, tracking upload requirements, carrier label rules, and integration needs.

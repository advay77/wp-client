 The DTC Fulfillment Workflow 
 
 
Every DTC setup, whether garage or enterprise, follows the same chain: 
 
 
1. Receiving: Supplier or manufacturer shipments arrive; units are counted, inspected, and logged in the WMS. 
 
 
2. Storage: Inventory is slotted by SKU (and lot, if expiration-dated). 
 
 
3. Order ingestion: Orders sync from Shopify or your OMS to the fulfillment system automatically. 
 
 
4. Pick and pack: Warehouse staff pick units, pack to your spec (poly mailer, box, branded insert). 
 
 
5. Ship: Carrier label generated; tracking pushed back to the customer. 
 
 
6. Returns: RMA received, inspected, restocked or disposed per your rules. 
 
 
Example: A skincare brand receives 2,000 serums from their manufacturer. Each bottle is logged with lot and expiration. When a customer orders on Shopify, the WMS routes the pick to the oldest eligible lot (FEFO), packs in a branded mailer, and uploads tracking to Shopify within the same-day cutoff.

 Technology Setup: Store â†” Warehouse Connection 
 
 
Broken integrations cause oversells, wrong items, and silent SLA misses. 
 
 
Minimum requirements for 2026 DTC setup: 
 
 
- Real-time order sync from Shopify (or OMS) to WMS,not nightly CSV 
- Inventory sync back to storefront so stock counts stay accurate 
- Tracking upload to customer notifications automatically 
- SKU/barcode match between catalog and warehouse (leading cause of mispicks) 
- Lot/expiration fields if you sell supplements, food, or cosmetics 
 
 
Onboarding test: Run 50â€“200 test orders before live cutover. Deliberately test multi-item orders, bundles, and a return. 
 
 
Since Shopify shut down its Fulfillment Network (SFN), merchants choose their own stack: Shopify Shipping for small in-house volume, or a 3PL app/API integration at scale. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Multi-Channel DTC: Shopify + TikTok + Marketplaces 
 
 
Most ecommerce brands in 2026 are not DTC-only. They sell on their site and TikTok Shop, Amazon, or Walmart. Setup mistakes happen when one warehouse treats all channels identically. 
 
 
Architecture principles: 
 
 
1. Define channel priority , Which channel gets inventory first when stock is tight? 
2. Set safety stock buffers per channel 
3. Use FEFO for expiration-dated products , TikTok velocity can push wrong lots out if unmanaged 
4. Separate prep workflows , FBA needs FNSKU; WFS needs UPC; DTC needs branded pack; TikTok needs scan-speed

 Branded Experience: Packaging, Kitting, and Subscriptions 
 
 
DTC fulfillment is also a brand touchpoint,unlike marketplace fulfillment where platform rules dominate. 
 
 
Document in your 3PL SOP: 
 
 
- Box vs mailer rules by SKU 
- Insert and sample policies 
- Gift note capability 
- Kitting/bundle assembly steps 
- Subscription box sequencing (if applicable) 
 
 
If branded unboxing is core to retention, verify the 3PL has done similar work at your volume,not just B2B pallet picks.

 Common DTC Setup Mistakes 
 
 
1. Choosing on headline pick/pack only , storage minimums surprise you month two 
2. No barcode/SKU audit , phantom inventory within days of go-live 
3. Shared inventory without rules , TikTok or Amazon flash sales wipe Shopify stock 
4. Skipping returns SOP , damaged units re-enter circulation 
5. No written SLA , liability capped at product cost when a $50k pick error happens 
6. Founder stays in the loop manually , scaling requires systemized exception handling 
 
 
 
 
 FAQ 
 
 
 Frequently Asked Questions 
 
 
What is DTC fulfillment? 
DTC (direct-to-consumer) fulfillment is storing, picking, packing, and shipping orders from your brand directly to individual customers,typically from orders placed on your own ecommerce site or app, without a retailer intermediary. 
 
 
When should an ecommerce brand outsource DTC fulfillment? 
Common triggers include exceeding roughly 500â€“1,000 orders per month, rising pick errors, multi-channel complexity, national shipping cost pressure, or founders spending disproportionate time on packing orders instead of growth. 
 
 
How much does DTC fulfillment cost? 
Costs include receiving, storage, pick/pack, packaging materials, shipping, returns, and possible monthly minimums. Request an itemized rate card and model fully loaded cost per order for your SKU mix rather than trusting a single per-order quote. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
What is the difference between DTC fulfillment and a 3PL? 
DTC fulfillment is the function. A 3PL (third-party logistics provider) is a company that performs that function,and often marketplace prep,on your behalf from its warehouse. 
 
 
Can one 3PL handle DTC and Amazon FBA prep? 
Yes. Many operators pick Shopify orders to consumers and prep FBA or WFS inbound shipments from the same facility, but workflows, labels, and inventory pools must be managed separately. 
 
 
Do I need real-time Shopify integration with my 3PL? 
For scaling brands, yes. Real-time order and inventory sync reduces oversells and manual errors. Batch CSV exports fail when order volume spikes. 
 
 
What is hybrid DTC fulfillment? 
Hybrid fulfillment splits work,some SKUs or channels in-house, others at a 3PL,often to preserve branded touches on hero products while outsourcing high-volume standard picks. 
 
 
How long does 3PL onboarding take? 
Typical setup runs 4â€“6 weeks including integration, initial receiving, test orders, and partial go-live,longer for complex kitting, lot tracking, or multi-channel routing.

Walmart WFS Prep Requirements: A Guide for Sellers

Walmart Fulfillment Services has its own prep and packaging rules, and while they overlap with Amazon FBA in some places, treating the two programs as interchangeable is a common way sellers end up with rejected shipments. One meaningful difference stands out immediately: Walmart still offers a paid prep service, something Amazon discontinued in the US at the start of 2026. 
 
 
Summary: 
 
Walmart WFS requires every item to: 
 
 1. Carry a GTIN or UPC barcode 
 2. Be packaged in a sealed, damage-resistant container 
 3. Meet specific palletizing and labeling standards before shipment 
 
Sellers who can't meet these requirements in-house can use Walmart's own Prep Services program to outsource poly bagging and labeling for a fee. 
 
 
How WFS Prep Differs From Amazon FBA Prep 
 
 
The single biggest structural difference between the two programs is barcode strategy. Amazon increasingly requires seller-specific FNSKU labels for non-brand-registered sellers (see our article on FNSKU vs UPC for the details). 
 
 
Walmart takes a simpler approach: every item must be labeled with a GTIN or UPC barcode, and sellers without one can purchase a unique barcode through GS1. There's no Walmart-specific seller-attribution barcode equivalent to Amazon's FNSKU. 
 
 
The second major difference is that Walmart still runs its own Prep Services program. If a seller lacks the time, staff, or equipment to prep inventory correctly and consistently, WFS Prep Services will handle poly bagging and item labeling for a fee at the fulfillment center. 
 
 
This is functionally similar to what Amazon used to offer before ending the program in January 2026 and it means WFS currently gives sellers a fallback option that FBA no longer provides. 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
 
Core Item Requirements 
 
 
Before anything ships to a Walmart fulfillment center, each item needs to meet these baseline standards: 
 
 
 1. Sealed, closed packaging: items must be protected and ready for sale in a sealed or closed container 
 2. GTIN or UPC barcode on the outermost part of the packaging, positioned so it's scannable without opening the package 
 3. Sharp item protection: sharp or pointed items must be packaged so the edge can't cause injury during receiving, stocking, or transit, and outer boxes containing sharp items need to be labeled accordingly 
 4. Liquid containment: liquid items need packaging that prevents leaks throughout handling and shipping 
 
 
Poly bags aren't required for every item the way they often are with Amazon. Most single items with their own intact retail packaging and label don't need a poly bag. 
 
 
Bundled or kitted sets , for example, a bottle sold with a travel-size companion product , generally do need to be bagged together so they're received and sold as a single unit. 
 
 
Shipment Size and Weight Limits 
 
 
WFS accepts items up to 500 lbs, including packaging, with maximum dimensions of 120 x 105 x 93 inches. For products shipped in bulk, master cases typically need a GTIN-14 label , the 14-digit case-level barcode that identifies the quantity inside , separate from the unit-level barcode on each individual item. 
 
 
Palletizing Requirements 
 
 
For larger shipments, WFS has specific pallet standards: 
 
 
 1. Pallet size: 40 x 48 inch pallets only, GMA Standard Grade A, solid wood, with 4-way forklift access 
 2. Double-stacked pallets: maximum combined height of 108 inches 
 3. Stability: corner boards recommended, with stretch wrap applied to both layers on double-stacked pallets 
 4. Weight labeling: cases over 100 lbs need a "Mech. Lift" label, similar in purpose to Amazon's mechanical lift requirement 
 
 
Peak Season and Processing Time 
 
 
Under normal conditions, Walmart typically receives inventory within about 2 business days of delivery. 
 
 
That window extends , sometimes up to 10 business days , for items requiring unplanned prep work (such as incorrect labels), or during sales events, holidays, and Walmart's peak period from October 1 through December 31. 
 
 
Sellers planning Q4 inventory should build this slower receiving window into their timeline rather than assuming standard processing speed will hold through the holidays. 
 
 
What Happens When Prep Requirements Aren't Met 
 
 
Items that don't meet WFS packaging and labeling requirements may be repackaged or relabeled by Walmart for an additional fee, or rejected and returned at the seller's expense. 
 
 
This is a notable contrast to Amazon's current zero-tolerance approach , Walmart's Prep Services program means non-compliant items have a built-in correction path, though it isn't free, and relying on it as a habitual fallback adds cost and processing delay to every affected shipment.

Frequently Asked Questions 
 
 
What barcode does Walmart WFS require? 
Every item must be labeled with a GTIN or UPC barcode. Sellers without an existing barcode can purchase a unique one through GS1. Walmart doesn't require a seller-specific barcode equivalent to Amazon's FNSKU. 
 
 
Does Walmart still offer prep services for sellers? 
Yes. Walmart's Prep Services program allows sellers to outsource poly bagging and item labeling to WFS for a fee, which remains available even though Amazon discontinued its equivalent program in January 2026. 
 
 
What is the maximum weight and size for a WFS item? 
WFS accepts items up to 500 lbs including packaging, with maximum dimensions of 120 x 105 x 93 inches. 
 
 
What pallet type does Walmart require for WFS shipments? 
Walmart requires 40 x 48 inch pallets, GMA Standard Grade A, solid wood, with 4-way forklift access. Double-stacked pallets can't exceed 108 inches in combined height. 
 
 
How long does WFS take to receive inventory? 
Typically about 2 business days under normal conditions, extending up to 10 business days for items needing unplanned prep or during peak season from October 1 through December 31. 
 
 
Do all items need poly bagging for WFS? 
No. Most single items with intact retail packaging and a visible barcode don't need a poly bag. Bundled or kitted sets generally do need to be bagged together as one unit. 
 
 
What happens if my WFS shipment doesn't meet packaging requirements? 
Walmart may repackage or relabel the item for a fee, or reject and return the shipment at the seller's expense, depending on the severity of the issue.
